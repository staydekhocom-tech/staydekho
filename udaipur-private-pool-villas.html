What Makes a StayDekho Pool Villa Different

Most "pool villas" advertised in Udaipur are actually homestays with a shared or communal pool that other bookings can access at the same time. Every villa on this page is different: the pool is fenced or gated within the property boundary, and it belongs only to your booking for the dates you've reserved. That matters most for bachelor parties and family trips with young kids, where a shared pool with unknown guests simply isn't an option.

We also manage every one of these properties ourselves — there's a caretaker assigned to each villa, a direct WhatsApp line for support during your stay, and no reseller sitting between you and the actual owner. That's the same reason our prices tend to run lower than what you'd find for an equivalent villa on Airbnb or MakeMyTrip: those platforms add a 10–20% service fee on top of the owner's price, and we don't.

Best Time to Book a Pool Villa in Udaipur

October through March is peak season in Udaipur — cooler weather makes the pool and outdoor areas usable all day, and weekends fill up fastest, especially around long weekends and Rajasthani wedding season. April to June is hot but the pool becomes the main attraction rather than a bonus, and prices are usually easier to negotiate. Monsoon months (July–September) bring the Aravalli hills to life and our farm and jungle villas are at their most photogenic, though we'd recommend checking pool usability with us directly for that window.
