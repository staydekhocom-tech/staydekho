More Than Just a Booking

We started StayDekho because we saw a gap between the warmth of Indian hospitality and the cold experience of booking platforms. This is our story.

The Problem We Noticed

Planning a group trip shouldn't be a compromise

Have you ever noticed that when planning a trip for your entire family or a large group of friends, the biggest challenge is finding the right stay?

Usually, you face a tough choice: either split your group up into separate hotel rooms, or stretch your budget for a private luxury villa. But we noticed an even deeper issue in the travel industry: the booking platform is often someone else, and the actual host is someone entirely different.

In that gap between a third-party website and an unknown host, the warmth of service, trust, and guest experience can easily get lost. Seeing this firsthand in Udaipur, we knew something had to change.

How It All Began

Our Story

The idea, born in Udaipur

In 2026, we — Sanskar Patidar and Jayant Patidar — launched StayDekho. Our vision was big but simple: everyone deserves to experience a beautiful group stay at a price that feels right, without compromising on care and comfort.

We grew up in Udaipur — a city that has been welcoming guests for centuries and understands, perhaps better than anywhere else in India, that hospitality is not a service. It is a relationship.

Why we are different

There are thousands of booking websites out there, but StayDekho is built differently. We are not just a middleman. We directly manage all of our properties ourselves.

When the platform and the host are the same, our commitment is directly to you. By managing the properties firsthand, we ensure that every time you stay with us, you experience consistent quality, spotless spaces, and the genuine warmth of a home.

We have balanced premium stays with accessible pricing so that you never have to worry about a heavy bill — only about making memories.

"Hosting is a personal connection, not just a transaction. When you book with StayDekho, you aren't just reserving a space — you are stepping into a welcoming, stress-free environment." — Sanskar & Jayant Patidar, Co-Founders

Our promise to you

Whether it is a family reunion or a much-needed escape with friends, we have created spaces where your group can truly be together. Rooted in the rich hospitality of Udaipur, we believe every stay should feel personal, not transactional.
